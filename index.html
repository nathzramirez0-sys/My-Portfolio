Capstone · Android tablet app2026

FergBentables

An offline app for a showroom counter, now in use there. It keeps buyers, orders and stock: each order is followed to delivery and takes its items from the inventory, every payment is recorded (downpayments too), and it prints an order slip with the balance or a paid receipt. A thesis manager for college groups: chapters with versioned drafts, adviser feedback, a kanban task board, a group activity feed, push notifications, daily deadline reminders, a defense countdown and team stats, all working offline. About

Hello, I'm Jonathan Cercenia Ramirez.

I'm a full-stack developer and a Computer Science student at the University of Pangasinan – PHINMA. I like building software for the places it actually gets used: an office with no IT staff, a subscriber checking a bill on their phone, a showroom counter printing a receipt, a student finding a room on campus.

My thesis, Cable Manager, shows how I work; I was the full-stack developer in a team of five. It's billing and subscriber management for a local cable and internet provider: an office system that installs from one file and runs without the internet, a subscriber portal, and an Android app, all on one database. It has been in use at the provider's office since October 2026. Alongside it, I built the app for my team capstone, FergBentables, which is now in use at a showroom. On my own I've built ThesisFlow, a manager for thesis groups and their adviser, and three web projects, and I've worked as a programmer on three team projects for web and Android.

On the web I work in PHP, MySQL and JavaScript; on Android, in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, Room and Firebase. I care most about the parts that decide whether software survives real use: backups that restore completely or not at all, sign-ins that resist guessing, and tests that run with one command. Part-time work during my studies taught me to manage my time and see things through, and photo editing gives me an eye for how things look.
